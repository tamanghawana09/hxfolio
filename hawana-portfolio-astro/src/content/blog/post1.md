# Hello world

Welcome to my corner of the internet. This is where I will share thoughts on design, development, and everything in between, unfiltered and in my own words.

## Why I started this

Writing is thinking. Putting ideas into words forces clarity in a way that very little else does. This blog is that exercise, made public.

> The best time to start was yesterday. The second best time is now.

## What to expect

Expect posts about Astro, design systems, and the occasional deep dive into something I have been obsessing over. Sometimes they will be short notes. Sometimes they will be long essays.

Topics will include:

- Frontend development and Astro
- Design systems and typography
- Personal projects and experiments
- Things I am learning in public

## A note on consistency

I am not promising a schedule. I am promising honesty: when something is worth writing about, I will write about it.

Thanks for being here. More soon.
